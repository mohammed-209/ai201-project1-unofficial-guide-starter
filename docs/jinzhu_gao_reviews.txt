Summary:
Student reviews for Professor Jinzhu Gao are mixed. Some students describe her as nice, reasonable, funny, caring, and an easy grader. Positive reviews say her lectures are easy to follow, she tells students what to expect, exams can be open note and open book, and she provides a list of exam topics. Some students say her classes are easy if students participate, start labs early, and show effort.

Negative reviews focus mostly on communication and lecture clarity. Some students say she can be hard to understand because of her accent. Other students say she reads directly from slides, expects solutions to match the book, and does not explain questions well. COMP 173 and COMP 53 have both positive and negative reviews, so student experiences seem inconsistent.
