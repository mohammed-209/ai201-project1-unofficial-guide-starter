Summary:
Student reviews for Professor Afsoon Zowj are mostly positive, especially for COMP 051 and COMP 053. Students describe her as kind, approachable, helpful, and understanding. Some students say exams and midterms can be difficult, but grading can be lenient because she may round grades, drop a lower midterm, or offer extra credit. A newer COMP 047 review is much more negative and says the slides had incorrect or missing information and that lectures moved too quickly.
