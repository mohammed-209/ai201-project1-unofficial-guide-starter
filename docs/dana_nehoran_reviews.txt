Summary:
Student reviews for Professor Dana Nehoran are very mixed. Positive reviews describe her as inspirational, caring, respected, and a strong teacher for programming, analytics, data science, Python, R, and real-world scenarios. Some students say they learned useful skills, practiced data manipulation, and found the class interactive and practical.

Negative reviews describe her classes as difficult, fast-paced, and homework-heavy. Some students say she can be hard to understand because of her accent, moves quickly through material, and is difficult to access outside class. A few reviews say students without computer science background may struggle, especially in COMP 051 or similar introductory programming courses. One 2025 review makes serious negative claims about a startup-related opportunity and warns students to be careful.

Useful Student Advice:
Students should get help early, especially if they have little coding background. Students should expect programming-heavy work in courses like COMP 051, COMP 061, and COMP 162. For data science or analytics courses, students may learn useful real-world skills in Python, R, data manipulation, and analytics. Students who struggle with fast-paced lectures should use office hours and ask questions early.
