Summary:
Student reviews for Professor Cathryn Carlson are very positive. Students describe her as sweet, helpful, organized, clear, responsive, and caring. Most reviews focus on COMP 025, where students say assignments are straightforward, grading criteria are clear, and the class is manageable if students attend, pay attention, and complete the work.

Students often mention that COMP 025 is useful for beginners, especially for Excel, HTML, practical computer skills, websites, and basic technology work. Reviews describe exams and quizzes as straightforward, especially when students review the PowerPoints or pay attention to lectures. Some students say the course includes busy work, labs, homework, projects, and online lectures, but most say the workload is manageable.
