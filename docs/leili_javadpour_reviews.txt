Summary:
Student reviews for Professor Leili Javadpour are mostly positive. Students often describe her as kind, caring, approachable, respectful, and helpful. Many reviews mention that she explains material clearly, gives useful examples, answers emails quickly, and is accessible outside of class. Several students say she gives good feedback and makes difficult material easier to understand.

Some reviews are less positive. One student said she changes the course plan too often. Another said she did not provide enough support near the end of the semester. A Numerical Methods review says the class was very difficult for a student who was not strong in calculus, MATLAB, or differential equations.
